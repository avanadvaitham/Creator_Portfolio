A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before we create.

Can I book UGC without posting to your account?+

Yes—UGC creation and influencer posting are scoped separately. Tell me where the content will be used so that production and licensing can be quoted correctly.

Are paid-ad usage and raw files included?+

Not by default. Paid usage, partnership ads, raw footage, exclusivity and extended licence periods need an explicit written agreement and a separate quote where applicable.

Which languages can we discuss?+

Telugu, Hindi, English, Hinglish. Telugu-first creative can be adapted for the agreed audience. Final wording and timing should be reviewed before filming.

How are results and claims presented?+

No views, sales or outcomes are guaranteed. Any performance figures shared in a pitch should be dated and supported by the relevant source. Product claims must be supportable and approved for the brief.

What happens when I prepare a brief here?+

The brief builder runs on your device. It does not submit or store an enquiry on this server. Copy or download it, or send it using the business contact when available. Instagram or YouTube content loads only after you request it and is subject to that platform’s policies.
